# Proposed alternative: two persistent reading cards

The [cards prototype](cards/README.md) explores replacing the visible dividing line with two subtly green/blue cards and a narrow draggable gap. Open [cards/index.html](cards/index.html) locally to try it. Each card retains its independent appending log; resizing never changes text size.

This is an additive design experiment prompted by the next conversation proposal. The original portrait prototype, canonical specification, decisions, archive, tests and mockups are preserved unchanged. The cards variant is not yet an adopted product decision.

The cards folder includes the complete standalone demo, six separate vector previews, a browser PNG renderer, rationale, interaction/navigation rules, and 26 browser checks. Recording and translation are simulated. Its README records the remaining physical-device validation and production integration work.
