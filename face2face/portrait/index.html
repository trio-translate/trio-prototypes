A clickable interaction demo. No microphone, speech recognition or translation service is connected.

Drag the gap to share the space. Swipe either card to read its history. Tap for controls. Hold anywhere on your card to try recording.

Keyboard: Tab to a card, Enter for controls, hold Space to record. Arrow keys resize the focused gap. Escape cancels or goes back.
